## 🎮 About the Game

The Lord of the Rings: War in the North™ - Legacy Edition is a third-person action RPG and mod-and-slash adventure developed by Aspyr Media. Released on August 11, 2026, it revisits the original 2011 game developed by Snowblind Studios. The campaign follows Eradan, Andriel, and Farin as they defend Middle-earth against Agandaur's forces, with single-player and cooperative multiplayer support.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **The Lord of the Rings: War in the North™ - Legacy Edition**. This trainer provides configurable support for health, power, ammunition, consumables, experience, gold, skill points, cooldowns, combat damage, and Hero Mode during offline play.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to adjust campaign difficulty, experiment with Eradan, Andriel, and Farin builds, explore equipment and skill trees freely, or reduce repetitive resource and cooldown management during offline play.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Health** — Keeps the active hero's health from being depleted during battles.
- **Unlimited Power** — Prevents power from being consumed when using character abilities and spells.
- **Unlimited Ammunition** — Prevents arrows and bolts from being reduced while using ranged weapons.
- **Unlimited Consumables** — Preserves health potions and power potions after use.

### 📌 Progression

- **Experience Multiplier** — Increases experience gained from defeating enemies and completing campaign encounters.
- **Skill Points** — Provides additional skill points for unlocking and improving abilities in each hero's skill tree.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Gold Modifier** — Changes the amount of gold available for purchasing equipment, potions, ammunition, and other supplies.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **No Skill Cooldowns** — Removes or reduces waiting time between uses of unlocked combat skills.
- **Hero Mode** — Maintains or fills the Hero Mode meter to enable stronger attacks and ability effects.
- **One-Hit Defeat** — Allows regular enemies to be defeated with a single successful attack.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Move through the campaign using the standard keyboard and mouse or controller controls. The game uses a third-person perspective, objective markers, a compass, and structured story areas rather than a fully open world. Switch between melee and ranged attacks as the situation requires, and use the trainer only after the active character has loaded into gameplay.

### Tools & Inventory

Open the Inventory menu to manage equipment, items, skills, quest information, and statistics. Characters collect weapons, armor, accessories, ammunition, health potions, and power potions. Equipment can be exchanged between the three heroes, while the trainer can preserve consumables or modify gold and progression resources.

### World & Environment

The campaign follows the fellowship through locations including Bree, Rivendell, Mirkwood, the Grey Mountains, and other northern regions of Middle-earth. Complete objectives, search areas for loot, rescue allies, and use character-specific abilities to reveal or access hidden areas. Save the game before changing progression or inventory values.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat combines light and heavy melee attacks, blocking, dodging, ranged weapons, character abilities, and power-based skills. Eradan specializes in ranger combat and archery, Andriel uses a staff and offensive or defensive magic, and Farin relies on heavy weapons, shields, and close-quarters strength. Consecutive attacks build the Hero Mode meter, which increases combat effectiveness. Activate trainer options selectively during combat and disable major damage or invulnerability options before scripted encounters when you want normal progression.
